FAQ

Straight answers.

What counts as a project?

One finished lip sync video. Upload a face, add audio, get an MP4: that is one project. If a render fails on our side, it is not counted.

Are there really no credits?

None. You pay a flat monthly price and get a fixed number of projects for that month. There is no balance to watch and nothing to top up.

What happens when I hit my monthly limit?

You can upgrade and keep creating right away, or wait until your count resets on your next billing date. Everything you already made stays in your library.

What do I need to upload?

A face and a voice. The face can be a photo (JPG, PNG, WEBP) or a video (MP4, MOV, WEBM). The audio can be MP3, WAV or M4A, or you can record in the browser. Clear, front-facing faces give the best result.

Which languages work?

Whatever is on the recording. The sync follows the sound, so the language is set by your audio, not by a menu.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Yes, on every plan. You are responsible for having the rights to the face and the voice you upload.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from your account. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.
